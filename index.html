Assumptions

Casual pay is what a working fortnight brings in; the slider is how many fortnights you expect to actually work. Fixed costs come from config (rent, bills, the B set-aside). Eating-out and giving targets are yours to set — they don't affect the runway maths, they're what the weekly trackers measure against.

What changed

Movements in the smoothed trends since the last check-in. The trend needs several consistent periods to move, so a single big week nudges it rather than yanking it.

Burn

Burn is money that left Up for good, net of what was paid back to you, split by what it was for. Jar-to-jar transfers don't count, except the fortnightly B set-aside. Fortnight and Month views show days with a 7-day rolling average; longer views show weeks with a 3-week half-life trend and its flux range. Rent was higher before the July move.

Runway

How long each pot lasts at the current trend. The living pot is your everyday money and assumes the casual income you set above; the fun jars are drawn down by fun spending only. The flag is your target date; the faint bar is the best-to-worst range from the flux in the burn estimate.

Freedom progress

The freedom number is annual spend × 25 — roughly the balance at which investment returns could cover your spending indefinitely. It moves with your spending, so trimming a habit lowers the target as well as raising the savings. Super is included in progress even though you can't touch it yet.
